# Opportunity Discovery And Screening Losses

Use for requested candidate discovery or screening, not as an extra stage in every company report. A candidate worth investigating has not yet passed investment underwriting.

## Define What The Search Can Find

State the authorized universe, information cutoff, data coverage and material uncovered parts. Separate explicit user constraints from provisional ranking heuristics. Respect hard constraints; do not substitute adjusted profitability for a requested reported-profit threshold. A soft profitability, growth or ownership filter may be a useful starting point without being a universal definition of quality.

Combine broad screening, relevant operating events and leads from related-company research only where they serve the request. Do not default to exhaustive global coverage, unsolicited outreach, paid data or persistent monitoring. Public tips are leads to verify, not independent evidence merely because several people repeat them.

## Inspect What The Filter Discards

When an early filter could hide relevant opportunities, inspect a proportionate sample of near-threshold and excluded candidates. Check missing or stale fields, accounting differences, one-off items and business-stage effects against original evidence. Missing data, a failed user constraint and a failed economic mechanism are different exclusion reasons. A temporary loss may warrant investigation; it does not establish normalized profitability or investment merit.

Keep enough dated reasons in the existing research output to explain exclusions and reopen provisional ones when specified evidence changes. Do not permanently discard a candidate merely because it is currently unattractive, nor keep researching one that clearly violates the requested scope. Broader candidate generation must still lead to strict checks of economics, valuation, financing, governance and per-share outcomes before an investment conclusion.

## Evaluate Search Quality Without Hindsight

For repeated search or a claimed screening improvement, compare the same point-in-time universe and total research effort, including validation of weak candidates. Retain selected and unselected cases and the information then available. Assess useful-candidate yield, inspected false exclusions and downstream research burden, not raw candidate count or only subsequent winners. Full recall is unknown unless the relevant population has been independently assessed; a sample audit supports only its covered scope. A one-off screen needs an honest coverage statement, not a new backtest or registry.
