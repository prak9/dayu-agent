# Mode D — Serenity Alpha (News To Financial Statements)

Use when material news, a product launch, technology change, procurement clue or earnings-call signal is supplied or discovered during company research, or for requested beneficiary searches. In default multi-lens research, proactively investigate relevant changes and integrate their financial transmission into Mode A; do not expand a single-company task into a universe search.

Start with this chain:

```text
news -> observed signal -> economic hypothesis -> rival explanations
-> independent corroboration -> net cash-flow transmission
-> company-scale value effect -> validation path
```

## Workflow

1. **Separate talk from economic evidence.** For a demand thesis, prefer paid adoption, deployments, shipments, backlog, price, lead time and production schedules, while testing their conversion into end demand. Cost, regulation, capital allocation or negative signals need their own transmission evidence, not a forced demand-growth story. Management commentary establishes a claim to test, not independent validation. Keep an unverified benefit as a research hypothesis. Cluster orders, shortages, price rises and commentary caused by one event; they are not independent confirmations.
2. **Translate into financial lines.** Map units, ASP, mix, customer count, usage, take rate, backlog conversion, capacity, input cost, utilization, opex, working capital, capex, and reporting lag. Check competition, substitution, qualification, delivery, financing, currency, M&A, and accounting alternatives.
3. **Rank business purity and net elasticity.** Trace how much of the change survives customer bargaining, competitive response, incremental cost, working capital, capex and dilution. Direct exposure or a small revenue base does not prove shareholder upside. Match validation to actual contract, qualification and reporting lags; 1–4 quarters may be useful for near-term signals, not a universal cutoff.
4. **Test the pricing dispute when valuation is in scope.** Recover a dated observed expectation or conditional price hurdle, and check whether it already includes the news. Quantify the supported incremental value with duration and offsets; distinguish an interpretation gap from new facts. Explain misclassification or neglect only when evidence supports it. A relabeling story alone is not alpha; a binding contract can provide evidence before reported revenue, subject to performance and collection risk.
5. **Build the evidence chain.** State which original observation supports each causal link and its strongest rival, including negative or overlapping evidence across company, customer, supplier and competitor sources. Define observable discriminators and economic breakpoints; do accessible follow-up checks now and identify genuinely unavailable evidence.
6. **Score only when useful.** Qualitatively or 1–5 compare signal support, transmission clarity, purity, company-scale elasticity, neglect, validation speed, and downside. Do not average away a broken transmission link.
7. **Set a research posture.** Observe, consider after specified proof, or reject the supported claim as appropriate. A small position does not validate an uncertain thesis, and proof of an unfavorable value gap does not establish executable short-sale economics. Do not force a candidate, a numeric score or a trade from a news request.

## Neglect And Historical Winners

Use these checks only when small size, low coverage, illiquidity or future institutional discovery carries the opportunity thesis; they are not a preference for microcaps or a mandatory screening stage.

- **Explain the absence.** Distinguish overlooked economics or documented mandate/position-size constraints from a justified discount for weak governance, funding dependence or poor business quality. Institutions have different eligibility rules; do not assume none can buy. Name what could change a relevant constraint and what supports that path, without making an imminent rerating necessary by default.
- **Locate the scarcity and its expiry.** Separate hard-to-replace operating capabilities, scarce eligible listed exposure for a defined buyer group, and limited shares available for trading. Customer substitution tests the first; competing listings or changes in investment mandates test the second; issuance, unlocks, conversion and holder sales test the third. Distinguish total, diluted and free-float shares from actual willingness to sell; low turnover alone identifies neither a moat nor fixed supply. Higher prices can encourage new supply. Trace the supported supply response and timing alongside buyer demand, allowing scarce listed exposure to weaken while business economics improve. Low institutional ownership limits that group's existing selling inventory; other selling, funding and operating risks remain.
- **Value without discovery.** Test whether supported per-share cash generation and distributions can justify the case without new institutional buyers or multiple expansion. If returns depend on discovery, expose that dependence. Carry financing and dilution into the model, and spread, executable capacity and stressed exit liquidity into the applicable exposure analysis; low liquidity is both a possible source of mispricing and a real cost, not free alpha.
- **Learn from matched outcomes.** When using past winners to infer a selection edge, compare contemporaneously similar failures and ordinary outcomes using information available at the selection date. The share of winners that began small is not the probability that a small company becomes a winner. Before importing historical size/liquidity returns, inspect the universe, delistings, survivor selection, weighting, rebalancing, sample window and trading costs. If the underlying study cannot be checked, retain the claim as unverified rather than a return assumption. Do not require a new backtest for every company memo.

## Output

Lead with the supported effect and its magnitude, duration, offsets and evidence limits. A narrow cost or accounting question may need only the calculation and its assumptions. For a research thesis, add the causal chain, strongest rival and discriminating check; add price requirements only when relevant. Candidate searches may compare direct and indirect exposures where supported, but do not require three beneficiary tiers, a primary pick, scores or an exposure recommendation. If no investable gap is demonstrated, say so. Keep source links with the claims they support.
