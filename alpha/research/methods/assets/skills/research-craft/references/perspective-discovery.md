# Perspective-Driven Evidence Discovery

Use when an unfamiliar topic, one-sided evidence or a material disagreement leaves a consequential question unexamined. This is an optional exploration lens, not a fixed sequence, role count, report template or requirement to delegate. Reuse the current question, evidence record and authorization.

## Use Perspectives To Find Evidence

A perspective identifies questions and places to look; a hypothesis proposes an explanation; evidence tests it. Select perspectives for their access to different information or constraints, not their ability to sound like different experts. Derive them from the actual actors, mechanisms and promising source material rather than a universal practitioner/academic/skeptic roster.

For a selected gap, connect the unanswered question to an accessible source and the claim it could change. For example, an AI-cloud supplier can document equipment delivery, but customer usage, billing and renewal evidence address whether that capacity produces recurring demand. Neither source alone establishes the other link.

Retrieve relevant original passages and use their answers to refine the next question. Reuse valid evidence already obtained. A simulated expert's answer is a candidate idea, not an interview, observation or independent source. Different roles can inherit the same mistaken premise; trace reports to their underlying origin rather than treating repeated agreement as corroboration. Agreement may be informative when independently supported, but role voting adds no such support. An omitted perspective is a gap in this investigation, not proof of a field-wide research gap.

## Resolve The Kind Of Disagreement First

Align the object, period, population, metric definition, denominator and conditions before declaring a conflict. Distinguish:

- **Fact conflict:** comparable claims cannot both hold; inspect the original observations and measurement quality.
- **Scope or definition difference:** reconcile or keep the claims separate; do not choose a winner or average incomparable values.
- **Mechanism disagreement:** observations fit competing explanations; identify a differential prediction and the cheapest discriminating evidence.
- **Goal or interest difference:** actors value different outcomes; make the tradeoff explicit rather than claiming more factual research will select the user's priorities.

For example, falling customer prices and rising supplier margins can coexist if unit costs fall faster. They are not automatically opposing accounts. Incentives motivate scrutiny, not automatic rejection; weigh evidence rather than granting each side equal credibility.

For a material unresolved dispute, retain the specific claims, their source support, what would distinguish them and the effect on the requested conclusion. Reuse the existing record; no separate contradiction map is required. Where useful, the mechanism-testing details are in [hypothesis formation](hypothesis-formation.md).

## Synthesize Without Inventing Certainty

Let findings revise the outline instead of forcing retrieved facts into a predetermined thesis. Audit both the supporting facts and the inference connecting them: two verified observations do not verify an asserted causal link. Omit unsupported "hidden connections", novelty claims, findings quotas and action slots. An unresolved finding can be the correct deliverable.

Use self-review to locate claims needing evidence, not to certify reliability. Do not replace source checks with uncalibrated numeric confidence, imagined professor grades or simulated peer-review approval. State the evidence, remaining uncertainty and the accessible check that could change the claim; independent review is not created by assigning the same model another role.

Stop expanding perspectives when additional sources are duplicates or cannot materially change the requested understanding or decision. If decisive evidence is inaccessible within scope, complete supported work and name the limitation. Do not seek permission for an already authorized lookup, expand access rights or require exhaustive coverage of every stakeholder.
